A PEACEFUL LOOP

The Green Hand Initiative is a concept gameplay loop focused on giving plants more purpose in general. It adds a peaceful and pleasing layer to your underground life, giving a new purpose to your extractions and providing a lore-friendly way to actively support Speranza's survival!

This concept also emphasizes giving projects AN ACTUAL PURPOSE.

HELPING SPERANZA


Celeste heard that raiders are now growing their own plants and has established a new program: the green hand initiative, a milestone-type system based on your total number of crops donated ! Earn Merits and buffs through her new program, and and help rebuild and reinforce Speranza.
